LexUK — Tenancy Law Chatbot

A retrieval-augmented, agentic chatbot grounded in twelve UK tenancy statutes. Answers tenant and landlord questions with citations to the relevant Act and section, so the user can verify every claim against primary law — not against a model's guess.

  • Retrieval-augmented generation over a curated statute corpus.
  • Agentic reasoning loop with tool-use for statute look-up.
  • Citation-first responses to keep legal advice traceable.
